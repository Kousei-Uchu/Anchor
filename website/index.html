    What Anchor is

    A private companion for iPhone and iPad that keeps what you need on hard days in one place, written by you, ready when you are.

    Now

    Crisis numbers for your region, a gentle check-in, your people, and grounding tools you wrote yourself.

    Plan

    Safety plan, contacts, trigger windows, appointments, de-escalation scripts and affirmations.

    Journal

    A private timeline with Markdown, photos and drawings, with optional suggestions from your calendar, photos and music.

    Bridge

    Prepare for appointments, keep a list of things to raise, and export notes if you choose to share them.

    Thread

    A message log, notes to your future self, things you haven't said yet, and a support calendar.

    Widgets

    Lock screen and home screen shortcuts to your top contacts, so you don't have to open the app.

    Anchor is not finished.

    This is an early build. Screens, features and wording may change, things may break, and what you see here may not match the app yet.

    The one thing that won't change

    • Anchor sends no data anywhere. Everything you write stays on your device.
    • If a future feature ever truly needs to send something, it will be the minimum necessary.
    • If that ever changes, you will be told immediately, in the app, before anything is sent.
