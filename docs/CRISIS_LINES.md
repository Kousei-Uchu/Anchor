# Crisis lines

The Crisis numbers card on Now shows each region's standard lines. In Settings > Crisis numbers the person can switch on extra lines
for LGBTQIA+ people and for the region's Indigenous peoples, and add numbers of their own. **Find a helpline** is always on the card.

A line is only listed when it was checked against its own organisation's page, a government page, or several agreeing sources.
Where nothing could be checked, nothing is listed and there is no switch, rather than an unchecked number. Checked on **26 November 2026**.
Phone numbers change, so recheck these every few months.

## Left out on purpose

- **United States, 988 "Press 3"** for LGBTQ+ young people was discontinued in July 2025, so it is not listed. The Trevor Project line is.
- **United States, Native and Strong Lifeline (988, press 4)** only serves Washington state, so it is not listed as a national line.
- **United Kingdom, Ireland and New Zealand, Indigenous lines:** none checked, so none listed and no switch.
- The standard lines for each region (Emergency, the national suicide lines) are unchanged.
