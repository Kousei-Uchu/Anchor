# What's stored

Settings > Your data and help > What's stored lists every kind of thing Anchor keeps, with how many there are. It is behind Face ID
(or the passcode) when the app lock is on, and has a switch to blur what is written.

## Looking and deleting (Patch DM1)

- Open a kind to see its entries, search them, and open one to read every field.
- **Delete one:** swipe an entry, or open it and choose Delete. Both ask first.
- **Delete several:** press Edit, tick the entries (Select all is there too), and choose Delete. It asks first.
- A delete does what deleting it from its own screen does: a Journal entry's voice notes go with it; a Recovery counter takes its days and urges
  and remakes its reminders; a safety plan section takes what sits under it; reminders for contacts, appointments, routines and trigger windows
  are remade from what is left; and the widgets are refreshed.
- Deleting is permanent, and from iCloud too if sync is on. Nothing is kept in a bin.
- Nights read from Apple Health are not listed, because Anchor does not keep them.

## Not here, on purpose

- **Creating and editing.** Every kind already has its own screen for that, and a second, plainer way to edit would skip the checks those screens do
  (what must not be blank, what is locked, what reminders to remake). To change something, use its own screen.
