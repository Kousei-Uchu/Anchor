# Changelog

- Patch 28: a diagram can start from a template (a few loops and a map, from the plus menu), and the menu on a diagram's page can share it as a picture or a PDF, with or without the written reading; the files are made on the device and removed when the sheet closes.
- Patch 27: the free map is rebuilt: pan and zoom, undo and redo, arrows on links with an optional plus or minus and a few words, item colours, and linking by dragging from one item onto another. A loop can be opened as a free map.
- Patch 26: feedback-loop diagrams start as a guided loop: write what happens first, what it leads to and so on, mark each link as more leading to more or more leading to less, and the app says in plain words whether the loop tends to keep itself going or to settle back. Free maps are still there from the plus menu.
- Patch 25: Presets has a Lists side: make a checklist with headings, set numbers like Day or Person, and let an item's amount follow one (for example 1 per day plus 1 extra for every 3), so changing the days updates every amount; reset the ticks to reuse a list, or make a copy.
- Patch 24b: a routine can have a reminder on the days and time you pick: it is off until you switch it on, in the routine or in Settings under Routine reminders, and the lock screen only says your routine is ready, never which one.
- Patch 24: Presets has a Routines side next to Decisions: a routine is a named list of steps, each with an optional rough time, that you run one step at a time; routines you have made also show on the Decision presets widget.
- Patch 23: a Quick list (in the Tasks menu) is a one-off checklist: type or paste one item per line, tick them off, add more as you go, and it moves to an Archive by itself when the last one is ticked; you can also move what is left into your tasks, start a list again, or delete it.
- Patch 21b: the focus timer can show on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island, with the phase and a live countdown but never the task; it is off until you switch it on in the timer's settings or in Settings.
- Patch 22: a task can have a rough time estimate, and Plan my day (in the Tasks menu) lets you say how much time you have, pick tasks and steps, put them in order and go through them one at a time, with Focus on this, Done and Not now on each.
- Patch 21: a task or step can be worked on with a focus timer: hold it and choose Focus on this, or open Focus timer from the Tasks menu; set the lengths, breaks and rounds with the gear; a bar on the Tasks screen shows it while it runs; alerts when a phase ends are off until you turn them on, and a task keeps a plain count of the sessions that ran to the end.
- Patch 20: on Plan, Bridge and Thread, Customise (under the widgets, or hold a widget) lets you arrange them where they are: drag one onto another to move it, tap the minus to hide it and add it back from the Hidden row, make a half-width widget full width, and Reset to start again.
- Patch 19: a task can have a tiny first step, say how much energy it needs, be marked started or set aside for now, and be moved to tomorrow with a swipe; the Tasks list can show only the lighter ones on a low day, and Now and the Tasks widget leave out whatever is set aside.
- Patch 18: a task can wait on other tasks: it moves to "Waiting on something else", dimmed and showing what it is waiting on, and goes back to its usual place when those are ticked off; Now and the Tasks widget show only what can be done now.
- Patch 17: a task can have steps: add them in the task's editor, tick them on the list, fold them away with the chevron, and see "2 of 5 steps" with a small bar; ticking every step leaves the task for you to finish.
- Patch 14b: a message can carry a voice note: record it when you write, play it from the log, send it through Messages as an attachment, or share it anywhere; it stays on your device and is removed when you delete the message.
- Patch 14a: the Message log has a "Write to" row so writing to a person is one tap from the log, a menu to show only one person's messages, and Send again and Copy on each message; the widget on Thread has a Write button.
- Patch 16: tasks can be timed loosely ("as soon as I can", today, this week, this month, someday) or for a particular day, and the list groups them as Today, This week, Later and No date; a plan whose time has passed moves quietly into Today, never marked as late.
- Patch 15: there is a Tasks list that lasts until you remove things: tick tasks off, tap one to add notes, add with the plus or the bar at the bottom, reorder, and optionally import from Reminders; Now shows your first few open tasks, and anything on the old "Next hour only" list is carried over once.
- Patch 13b: on Bridge, tap a draft item, your prep notes or a past session to write in it, add with the plus, delete from the long-press menu or by swiping; a new feedback-loop diagram opens straight away; a Journal entry opens in the editor when tapped; the old Edit button and list sheets are gone everywhere.
- Patch 13a: Affirmations, De-escalation scripts and Who I'm doing this for open in one sheet when tapped, add with the plus, delete from inside or from the long-press menu, and the first two have a labelled Reorder; "Before tracking" on the Streaks screen is tap to change.
- Patch 12a: on the Safety plan, tap a section to change it; on Trigger windows, tap a card to open it, use the plus to add, and delete from inside the card or its long-press menu; deleting a trigger window now also stops its reminders.
- Patch 12b: Contacts and Grounding tools open in one sheet when tapped, add with the plus, delete from inside or from the long-press menu, and have a labelled Reorder; a tool with steps has a play button on its card.
- Patch 12c: Decision presets: tap an option to edit it, tap a category name to rename it, add a category with the plus and an option from inside its card; steps have a labelled Reorder.
- Patch 12d: Appointments is one screen: tap a calendar day to schedule or open a session, use the plus for any date, and set the typical cadence right on the screen.
- Patch 11: on the Thread screens, tap a card to open it, use the plus to add, and delete from inside the card or its long-press menu; the Edit buttons and list sheets are gone.
- Patch 10: the shared pieces for tap a row to edit it, swipe to delete, a plus to add and a labelled Reorder are in place; no screen uses them yet.
- Patch 9: the Journal has a Year view with twelve small months; tap a month to open it.
- Patch 8: the Journal has a Days and Month switch; the month calendar shows a dot for each entry and a square for a check-in, and tapping a day opens it in the timeline.
- Patch 7: the Journal editor is one calm writing surface with a single Add button for Journaling Suggestions, photos, drawings, prompts and formatting; Preview moved into a menu; a prompt now adds to your entry instead of replacing it.
- Patch 6: the Journal timeline shows the newest day and the newest entry first, and a "Jump to today" action takes you back to the top.
- Patch 5: the check-in sliders are soft cards with an icon and end labels, the mascot's mood follows the sliders and changes per question, and nothing about this is scored or stored.
- Patch 4: each optional check-in question has a short line of reassurance under it and a row of sentence starters; the question screen scrolls and keeps its buttons pinned.
- Patch 3: check-in "Finish" and "A few optional questions" are pinned in a bottom bar so neither is hidden on small screens; the patch plan now lives in the repo as ANCHOR_PATCH_PLAN.md.
- Patch 2: long widget titles and captions are no longer cut off (up to three lines, then shrink); long-titled widgets go full width, and so do all widgets at accessibility text sizes.
- Patch 1: the current run is never shown as "0 days"; it steps down to hours or minutes, and the counter widget refreshes more often in the first day.
